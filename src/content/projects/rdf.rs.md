https://crates.io/crates/rdf_rs

RDF.rs is a Rust framework for working with RDF knowledge graphs. It
supports JSON-LD, Turtle/TriG, N-Triples/N-Quads, and more; implements
stores on top of SQLite, Valkey, MongoDB, IndexedDB, and others; and
interoperates with Oxigraph, Rudof, and Sophia.
