https://dydra.com

Dydra is a cloud-hosted, turnkey RDF triple store with a SPARQL query
engine, and indeed the very first graph database as a service (GDBaaS). It
[launched in February 2011][launch], [dominated benchmarks][benchmarks] at
the time, and landed S&P 500 customers such as NXP Semiconductors (whom it
helped win the [European Linked Data Award][award]).
