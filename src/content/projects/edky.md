https://edky.dev

Edky (pronounced _ed-key_) converts Ed25519 public keys between encoding
formats such as Base16, Base32z, Base58, Base64, Multibase, and OpenSSH.
It is available both as the command-line tool `edky` and as a polyglot
library.
